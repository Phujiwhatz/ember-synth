Five instruments: pick one with the tabs. Layer plays slot A and B together; Split puts B below the split point (press “Split at…”, then a key to move it). Edit A / Edit B chooses which slot the panel shows. Knobs and faders: drag up/down (Shift = fine), scroll, or arrow keys; double-click resets. MIDI: mod wheel adds vibrato (on the organ it switches the rotary speaker fast/slow), sustain pedal works, CC74/CC71 control each instrument's main tone knobs. ROLI Piano M / MPE: in ROLI Dashboard turn on pitch bend and pressure, use the Lower zone, and match Pitch Bend Range to “Note bend”. Web MIDI needs Chrome, Edge, Opera or Firefox.
