EMBER ULTIMATE — offline music studio for macOS
Ember Studio (5 synths) · Keys (sampled instruments) · Acid (303-style bass) · Drift (granular textures)

INSTALL
1. Drag "Ember Ultimate.app" into your Applications folder.
2. The first time, right-click it and choose Open, then Open again.
   (It isn't notarised by Apple, so macOS asks once.)
   If macOS says the app is damaged, open Terminal and run:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Ember Ultimate.app"

USE
- Works with no internet at all (made for flights). Everything is inside the app.
- Plug in your MIDI keyboard (ROLI Piano M works with MPE) before or after opening.
- Projects autosave. Use the record button to save what you play as a WAV.
